Stops the unit of a full id
$ wf stop 0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110
Stopping wf-0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110.service: session 0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110 has logged no prompt yet.

A prefix stops no unit
$ wf stop 0f1e2d3c
no session id starts with 0f1e2d3c

Reports a failed stop
$ wf stop 0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110
systemctl --user stop wf-0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110.service failed: Unit not loaded.

No session and no active unit
$ wf stop 0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110
no session id starts with 0f1e2d3c-4b5a-4968-8776-655443322110
